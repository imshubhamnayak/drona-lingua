S (Thin, sharp hiss)
Tongue Position
Tongue forward, close to the upper teeth ridge.
Airflow
Thin air through a small narrow gap. Sharp sssss sound.
Lip Shape
Lips relaxed / slightly smiling.
SH (Softer hush)
Tongue Position
Tongue slightly farther back and more rounded.
Airflow
Softer air through a wider channel. Gentle shhhhh sound.
Lip Shape
Lips slightly rounded.
Quick Memory:
S = Smile + Sharp thin air    SH = Round lips + Softer wider air
