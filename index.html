S vs SH
Most common confusion for UP/Bihar speakers
S (as in sun)
  • • Tongue forward, close to upper teeth ridge
  • • Thin, sharp air through narrow gap → sssss
  • • Lips relaxed / slightly smiling
SH (as in shoe)
  • • Tongue slightly back + rounded at front
  • • Softer air through wider channel → shhhhh
  • • Lips slightly rounded
Memory cue: S = Smile + Sharp   |   SH = Round + Soft
Z vs G
Another very common mix-up
Z (as in zoo)
  • • Tongue forward, near upper teeth
  • • Continuous buzzing sound (vocal cords vibrate)
  • • You can hold it: zzzzzz
G (as in go)
  • • Tongue back, touching soft palate
  • • Short stop sound — air is blocked then released
  • • You cannot hold it (guh)
Memory cue: Z = Continuous buzz   |   G = Stop + Release
