How to Make the Sound

Learn the correct tongue position, airflow, and lip shape first. This is the foundation.

S vs SH
Most common confusion for UP/Bihar speakers
S
S (as in sun)
Thin, sharp hiss • Voiceless
Tongue: Forward, close to upper teeth ridge
Airflow: Thin air through narrow gap → sssss
Lips: Relaxed or slightly smiling
SH
SH (as in shoe)
Softer hush • Voiceless
Tongue: Slightly back + rounded at front
Airflow: Softer air through wider channel → shhhhh
Lips: Slightly rounded
Memory: S = Smile + Sharp thin air   |   SH = Round lips + Softer wider air
Z vs G
Another very common mix-up
Z
Z (as in zoo)
Continuous buzzing • Voiced
Tongue: Forward, near upper teeth
Airflow: Vocal cords vibrate → continuous zzzzzz
Can hold it? Yes
G
G (as in go)
Short stop sound • Voiced
Tongue: Back, touching soft palate
Airflow: Blocked then released suddenly → short “guh”
Can hold it? No
